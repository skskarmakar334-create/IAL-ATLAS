The story behind the platform

About IAL ATLAS

A learning universe built to make education clearer, more accessible, and less confusing.

IAL ATLAS is a free, student-built educational platform created to help Edexcel International A Level students explore subjects, strengthen their understanding, and access organised learning resources in one place.

The idea is simple: difficult concepts should become easier to explore. Through subject-focused resources, learning atlases, revision materials, and digital learning tools, IAL ATLAS aims to make independent learning more approachable.

This project is built around the belief that students should have better access to learning resources, regardless of where they begin their educational journey.
