The idea behind IAL ATLAS

Learning should be clearer for everyone.

IAL ATLAS is a free, student-built educational project created to make learning resources easier to explore and understand. Its goal is to help students approach challenging topics with greater clarity, confidence, and independence.

The platform brings together subject-focused resources and learning tools in one place, with the aim of making self-study more accessible.
