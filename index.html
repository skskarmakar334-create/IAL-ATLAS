About the project

Built to make learning clearer.

IAL ATLAS is a free, student-built educational resource project founded and developed by Shuvojit Karmakar. Its goal is to make studying more approachable through organised learning materials, revision resources and educational tools.

IAL ATLAS is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially connected to Pearson Edexcel.

  • Free student-focused learning resources
  • Structured revision and subject materials
  • Designed to support conceptual understanding
  • Independent educational initiative

Learn more on the About page.
