Explore challenging ideas and ask for explanations in simpler language to build a stronger foundation.

📐

📝

🌌

Curiosity-Driven Learning

Go beyond memorization by asking why a concept works, how ideas connect and what assumptions a model uses.

Why IAL ATLAS AI exists

Technology should make learning more accessible.

IAL ATLAS is a free, student-built learning platform created to help Edexcel International A Level students navigate their studies with greater clarity.

IAL ATLAS AI extends that educational vision by providing an AI-assisted way to explore questions, revisit concepts and seek learning guidance.

The goal is not to replace teachers, textbooks or independent thinking. It is to help learners ask better questions, understand ideas more deeply and take greater ownership of their education.

Getting started

How to use IAL ATLAS AI

Start with a question, explain what you find difficult and use the response as a guide to further learning.

01

Open the AI Assistant

Visit the main IAL ATLAS website and open the AI assistant using the available navigation or AI section.

02

Ask a specific question

Identify the subject, topic or step that confuses you. Include the full question and any relevant information.

03

Request a clearer explanation

If something is unclear, ask for a simpler explanation, another example or a step-by-step breakdown.

04

Verify and practise

Compare important answers with trusted textbooks, official specifications and relevant past-paper mark schemes.

Important: AI-generated responses may contain mistakes. Always verify important academic information against reliable educational sources. Actual features and supported subjects depend on the AI assistant's implementation.
