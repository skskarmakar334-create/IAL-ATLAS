Mathematics

Algebra

Quadratic Formula

x = (-b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a
Used to solve quadratic equations of the form ax² + bx + c = 0.
a, b, c = coefficients of the quadratic equation
Coordinate Geometry

Gradient

m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
Determines the gradient of a straight line between two points.
Differentiation

Power Rule

d/dx (xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹
Fundamental differentiation rule for powers of x.

Physics

Mechanics

Newton's Second Law

F = ma
The resultant force acting on an object equals its mass multiplied by its acceleration.
F = force   |   m = mass   |   a = acceleration
Motion

Equation of Motion

v = u + at
Relates initial velocity, final velocity, acceleration and time.
Energy

Kinetic Energy

Eₖ = ½mv²
Energy possessed by an object due to its motion.

Chemistry

Amount of Substance

Moles

n = m / M
Calculates the amount of substance from mass and molar mass.
n = moles   |   m = mass   |   M = molar mass
Concentration

Concentration

c = n / V
Calculates concentration in terms of amount of substance and volume.
Gas Laws

Ideal Gas Equation

pV = nRT
Relates pressure, volume, amount, temperature and the gas constant.
