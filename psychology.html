Study smarter

How to study Psychology

Psychology is more than memorising definitions. Understand concepts, learn from evidence and practise applying ideas to examples.

📚 Build a strong answer

  • Define: Explain the key term accurately.
  • Explain: Describe the theory or process.
  • Evidence: Use relevant research when required.
  • Evaluate: Consider strengths, limitations and alternative explanations.
  • Apply: Link your answer to the question or scenario.

🧪 Research checklist

  • Identify the aim and hypothesis.
  • Distinguish the independent and dependent variables.
  • Identify the sample and sampling method.
  • Consider reliability, validity and possible bias.
  • Consider consent, confidentiality and protection from harm.
Exam tip: Check the command word. “Describe” asks what something is like; “explain” asks how or why; “evaluate” requires a balanced judgement supported by reasoning.
