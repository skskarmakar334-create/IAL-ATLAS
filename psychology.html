Study smarter

How to study Psychology

Psychology is more than memorising definitions. Learn theories, understand evidence and practise applying concepts to examples.

📚 Build a strong answer

  • Define: Explain the key term accurately.
  • Explain: Describe the theory or psychological process.
  • Evidence: Refer to relevant research when required.
  • Evaluate: Discuss strengths, limitations and alternative explanations.
  • Apply: Link your answer directly to the question or scenario.

🧪 Research methods checklist

  • Identify the aim and hypothesis.
  • Distinguish the independent variable from the dependent variable.
  • Recognise the sample and sampling method.
  • Consider reliability, validity and possible bias.
  • Discuss informed consent, confidentiality and protection from harm.
Exam tip: Always check the command word. “Describe” asks what something is like; “explain” asks how or why; “evaluate” requires a balanced judgement supported by relevant reasoning.
